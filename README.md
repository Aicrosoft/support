## Supported Products

- **[BookFun](https://aicrosoft.com/) (悦读)**: Unified e-book reader supporting all major formats (TXT, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, DOCX, Markdown, PDF, etc.) with 3D page flip effects, AI text-to-speech audiobooks, Markdown authoring (with formulas, charts, tables), and dual reading statistics.
- **[TrueCost Auto](https://aicrosoft.com/)**: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) estimation tool and vehicle purchase guide. Compare EV, hybrid, and gas vehicles by projecting depreciation, fuel/charging, insurance, maintenance, repairs, financing, taxes, and resale values over 1, 3, 5, or 10 years.
- **[FlashNote](https://aicrosoft.com/apps/flashnote/) (流光笔记)**: Sidebar Markdown distraction-free creation tool and browser extension (Chrome & Microsoft Edge) featuring AI assistance, web clipping, Mermaid diagrams, KaTeX formulas, knowledge-base compilation, and one-click static blog publishing.
- **[KinTime](https://aicrosoft.com/) (家时光)**: Cherish and track every minute and milestone of your baby's growth and family life with precise timers and memory logs.
- **[SavorDaily](https://aicrosoft.com/) (三餐四季)**: A poetic culinary journal to capture every bite and daily food memories.

## How to Get Support

We use **GitHub Issues** and **GitHub Discussions** to communicate with our users:

### 1. Issues (Bug Reports & Feature Requests)
If you encounter software failures, unexpected errors, or have concrete feature requests:
- **[Submit a Bug Report](../../issues/new)**:
  - App name and installed version.
  - Operating system and device model (e.g., iOS 17.4 / iPhone 15 Pro, Android 14 / Pixel 8, Chrome 124 on macOS).
  - Clear steps to reproduce the issue.
  - Screenshots, screen recordings, or error logs if available.
- **[Submit a Feature Request](../../issues/new)**:
  - Explain the problem or scenario you want to address.
  - Describe your proposed solution or feature in detail.

### 2. Discussions (Q&A & Community Chat)
For general questions, advice, sharing usage workflows, or brainstorming:
- **[Join GitHub Discussions](../../discussions)**:
  - **Q&A**: Ask how-to questions or troubleshoot configuration issues.
  - **Ideas**: Share new feature suggestions and brainstorm design ideas.
  - **General**: Connect with fellow users and the development team.
